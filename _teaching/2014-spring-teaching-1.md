I have had the opportunity to mentor both a high school student and a visiting research fellow in the Wang Lab, guiding them through the fundamentals of single-cell RNA-seq analysis and our lab’s computational pipeline. I developed onboarding materials, walked them through each step of the workflow and assigned an independent project that allowed them to apply these skills to real datasets. Throughout their training, I emphasized reproducible coding practices, clear documentation, and conceptual understanding of each analytical decision. Watching them gain confidence with the tools and contribute meaningful results to the lab strengthened my commitment to mentorship, and it remains one of the most rewarding aspects of my research experience.
