### **Bioinformatics Collaboration Overview**

I collaborated extensively with multiple UCSF Liver Center labs, providing computational support across single-cell RNA-seq, single-nucleus RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics, microRNA sequencing, proteomics, lipidomics, and ATAC-seq. My contributions ranged from experimental design consultation to full analytical pipeline development, integration, and interpretation.

---

### **Single-Nucleus RNA-seq of Mouse Liver HSCs (Tamoxifen-Induced Fibrosis Model)**

I led the snRNA-seq analysis of hepatic stellate cells (HSCs) to investigate how tamoxifen exposure influences fibrosis development. My objectives included: identifying differential gene expression between treatment groups, characterizing transcriptionally distinct HSC subpopulations, and inferring cell–cell communication between HSCs, hepatocytes, and macrophages. Because the study focused on collagen remodeling, I prioritized pathways involved in extracellular matrix turnover—evaluating markers such as *Mmp1*, *Mmp3*, and related regulators—to uncover transcriptional programs underlying fibrosis progression and resolution.

---

### **Spatial Transcriptomics (Visium HD) Integration With Single-Cell Data**

I performed Visium HD analysis of mouse liver samples (one wild-type, one knockout) to investigate metabolic remodeling in Kupffer cells. This included preprocessing, spot-level QC, spatial clustering, gene module detection, and integration with scRNA-seq reference datasets to resolve macrophage subtypes in spatial context. My analysis revealed how the knockout altered local inflammatory niches and reshaped Kupffer-cell metabolic pathways across the lobular architecture.

---

### **Proteomics of Normothermic Machine Perfusion (NMP) Liver Perfusate and Bile**

As the lead computational analyst and **second author**, I conducted an integrated proteomic analysis of bile and perfusate collected during NMP from human donor livers, comparing grafts that later developed early allograft dysfunction (EAD) versus those with normal function. Leveraging high-resolution quantification of ~11,000 proteins, I performed differential expression, pathway enrichment, and cross-fluid integration. Across both bile and perfusate, EAD grafts showed a convergent molecular signature of metabolic collapse, mitochondrial and ER stress, heightened inflammation, and loss of hepatocellular synthetic function. Reductions in protective hepatocyte-derived factors (e.g., HAMP, HPX, ANGPTL3) and endothelial markers (LYVE1, complement components) highlighted impaired microvascular and epithelial integrity. This work formed the basis of a full manuscript submitted to American Transplant Congress (ATC) 2026, where I contributed analytical design, figure generation, and biological interpretation.

---

### **ATAC-seq Analysis of MASLD vs. Healthy iPSC-Derived Hepatocyte Lines**

I compared chromatin accessibility profiles between iPSC-derived hepatocyte lines from MASLD patients and healthy controls to identify regulatory programs underlying metabolic dysfunction. My analysis included peak calling, differential accessibility testing, motif enrichment, and integration with transcriptomic data to identify transcription factors driving disease-specific epigenetic states. This work aimed to uncover how MASLD risk variants and metabolic stress reshape the hepatocyte chromatin landscape.
