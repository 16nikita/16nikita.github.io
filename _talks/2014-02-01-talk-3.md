**Abstract:** I presented an overview of liver gene analysis core services with a focus on results from our healthy liver Xenium spatial transcriptomics panel. I highlighted the panel’s success in identifying over 80 distinct cell types and its capacity to analyze multiple tissues simultaneously—processing approximately one million cells across 150 tissue samples using a tissue microarray. I also demonstrated our computational approach to sectioning tissue regions via graph-based methods, providing high-resolution spatial neighborhood analyses. These data serve as a resource for the UCSF Liver Center community, supporting collaborative research and advancing insights into liver physiology and disease mechanisms.
